Pro-Tips for a $20\text{ px}$ Body ScaleWhen you step up to a larger body font, two major layout factors change:Line Length (Measure): Because the characters are larger, your paragraphs will hit the edge of the screen much faster. For optimal reading comfort, a line of text should hold about 60 to 75 characters. Limit your paragraph container's width in CSS to around max-width: 42rem; or 680px. If lines get too wide, the reader's eye loses track when bouncing back to the next line.The Line-Height Sweet Spot: For a $20\text{ px}$ font, a pure Golden Ratio line-height ($1.618$) means your line-height evaluates to roughly $32\text{ px}$. This gives the text plenty of breathing room, turning blocks of copy into beautifully spacious, inviting text columns.
